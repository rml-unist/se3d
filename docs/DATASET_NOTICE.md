# SE3D dataset attribution

SE3D was generated with CARLA 0.9.15 and its urban scenes, buildings, vehicles
and other scene assets. Credit for CARLA and these assets belongs to the CARLA
project and its contributors. The
[CARLA 0.9.15 license statement](https://github.com/carla-simulator/carla/blob/0.9.15/README.md#licenses)
identifies CARLA-specific code as MIT and CARLA-specific assets as CC-BY.
Preserve upstream asset notices and their applicable license terms.

The CARLA code's copyright notice names Computer Vision Center (CVC) at the
Universitat Autonoma de Barcelona (UAB), 2017; its full terms are in the
[CARLA 0.9.15 code license](https://github.com/carla-simulator/carla/blob/0.9.15/LICENSE).

The SE3D authors' contributions are covered by
[DATASET_LICENSE.md](DATASET_LICENSE.md). Keep both notices when redistributing
the dataset.

CARLA research reference: Alexey Dosovitskiy, German Ros, Felipe Codevilla,
Antonio Lopez and Vladlen Koltun, *CARLA: An Open Urban Driving Simulator*,
CoRL 2017. [Paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v78/dosovitskiy17a.html).
